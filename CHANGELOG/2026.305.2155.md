# 2026.305.2155 — feat(bed): honour snapshot.force_stop on the keeper stop, with a bounded force fallback

## Summary

The golden-capture **keeper stop** in `runCheckBed` was a bare graceful `charly vm stop`: a desktop keeper that ignores ACPI past the stop grace left the venue unreleased for the whole 3m cap, and the step's own error named a remedy (`--force`) that the snapshot policy had **no field to carry** (opencharly/charly#934; the `force_stop` field itself landed in spec#244).

Three parts, in `candy/plugin-check/`:

1. **`keeperStopArgs`** appends `--force` to the emitted `snapshot-stop-keeper` step when the policy declares `force_stop`. Both forms release the same lock and both leave the venue **KEPT** — a force stop releases the *process*, not the domain definition.
2. **`keeperStopRetry`** is the ONE place the fallback is bounded: a **failed graceful** keeper stop is retried **once** with the forced argv; a failed **forced** one is not retried at all; and no other step ever falls back. A real failure is still reported instead of looped over.
3. The runner **names which path released the venue** (`graceful` / `forced`) in its note and its failure tail, so "how the lock was released" is never inferred from the absence of a timeout in a log.
