# 2026.317.1844 — fix(check): a deploy's add_candy refs ride the resolved-project request, so the child phase asserts the PINNED coordinate

Head `2d6603ba7d4c0ee9f9fbc0fb261be452589b8c61` — three commits, 14 files, +287/−18 (`git rev-parse HEAD`), branched from `origin/main` at `7e9dbd2`, 0 behind at body-writing time.

## Summary

`opencharly/plugin-check#112`: a bed's child `check live` phase composed its plan from a lookup keyed by the candy's **bare repo ref**, inside a resolution that never **materialized** the coordinate the deploy pinned — so a bed could advance its `add_candy:` pin, have it read and fetched correctly, and still not change what the phase asserted.

The mechanism, and why the fix is at the request rather than at the lookup: the resolved-project envelope fills `rp.CandyModels` from a scan whose remote-ref collection is **reachability-scoped over the project's image closure** (`sdk/loaderkit.CollectRemoteRefsOpts`), and a deploy's `add_candy:` refs sit outside that closure by construction. `candy/plugin-build`'s own resolved-project cache-key comment states the consequence in its own words — `ExtraCandyRefs` *"is the ONLY way a host-side plugin candy that no image closure reaches enters rp.Candies at all"*. Without the refs, the pinned coordinate is not a **candidate**, so the arbiter cannot pick it: `rp.CandyModels[bare]` carries whatever OTHER coordinate of that repo the closure reached (the `distro-arch` case: `layer-direnv:v2026.272.0057` reached transitively through `layer-agent-forwarding`, against the bed's `v2026.287.2155` pin), and since #113 the phase refuses naming both versions instead of asserting one.

This change threads the deploy's own refs into the request on the two arms that build an `add_candy:` plan:

- `pluginCheckRunLive` (`charly check live`) and `pluginCheckRunFeatureLive` (`charly check feature run`, whose VM arm reaches the same plan builder) now call `resolvedProject(ex, ctx, dir, addCandyRefsForEntity(ctx, ex, dir, req.Name))`.
- `addCandyRefsForEntity` owns exactly two facts: **which node** the deploy name denotes (over the plugin-side merged tree, with the host's own node-lookup contract — strip the `vm:` address prefix, exact full key first, then the dotted member walk, then the namespace-aware `uf.ResolveBed`) and **which scope** its refs take (`spec.BoxScope(name)`, because a deploy IS its box; a constant label is the #739 false-conflict bug). **The collection itself is `spec.DeployNode.ScopedAddCandyRefs`' one home** (`opencharly/spec#310`) — see R2.
- The envelopes that read the projection for **vocabulary / classification / venue only** (`resolveCheckProjection`, `venue.resolveCheckVenue`, `unresolved_classifier`, `score_live`) pass `nil`: they build no `add_candy:` plan, and widening them would only pay for fetches nothing reads.
- `candyAddSteps` keeps its identity-keyed lookup (that is what the envelope offers) and its fail-loud guard, whose doc comment now states what is true after this change: the guard is the narrow arm for a pin the newest-tag arbiter cannot satisfy, not the missing fix.

**Round 3 (head `2d6603b`) is the R2 cure and nothing else: no duplicated traversal remains in this diff.** It consumes `spec.DeployNode.ScopedAddCandyRefs(scope)` (landed as `opencharly/spec#310`) and deletes this package's copy, and it advances the `github.com/opencharly/spec` pin to the tag that PR minted.
