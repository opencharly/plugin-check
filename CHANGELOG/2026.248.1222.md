## SDD(d) — the hand-written `evidenceRow` mirror (exception documented, per the reviewer's ask)

`candy/plugin-check/evidence.go`'s `evidenceRow` mirrors `spec/schema/instrument.cue #EvidenceRow` as a hand-written Go struct instead of sourcing the generated `spec.Instrument`-style type. RCA: the generated `spec` type for `#EvidenceSegment` deliberately degrades to `[]map[string]any` because of its open-tail (`...`) CUE hatch (documented in spec#96's `instrument.cue`), and the evidence writer must mirror THAT degraded shape; additionally, `plugin-check` is a standalone module pinned to a spec version, and the evidence envelope travels as JSON-carried rows (the runner reads what providers write, per the session-service contract) — a hand-written mirror of a JSON-carried envelope keeps the runner independent of the schema-pin cadence (the same module-independence rationale as the plan's Cutover F consolidation note). The collector + writer are unit-tested (determinism tests in this PR). If the org prefers sourcing the generated type, that is the Cutover F consolidation surface.
