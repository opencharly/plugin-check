## Summary

**Leg 4 of unit 3's chain** (`TODO.md` 2.1, tracker `opencharly/spec#304`, row 3 — the `pod` kind). The contract module deleted `spec.Pod` (`opencharly/spec#307`, tag `v0.2026317.703`) and moved the authored `kind: pod` body into `plugin-substrate`'s own CUE module, and this plugin's include-splicer still decoded a `pod:` template into the deleted type:

