# 2026.254.1912 — fix(check): bound the post-restart venue reconnect with a total readiness deadline (G-3c)

## Summary

**RCA (routed from eval-omarchy PR #69's validator — the update-bed deadlock).** The restart-only `update_gate` power-cycles the bed's venue (`vm stop --force` → `vm start`, `candy/plugin-check/anchored.go` `updateGateSteps`) and the bed then **reconnects** to the rebooted guest. That reconnect went through `specexec.WaitForVmSshReady` (`bed_run.go` `waitReady`) — a **best-effort gate that is SILENT on timeout** and drives the readiness poll through `poll.WaitCapped(PollRemote, cap=0)`, i.e. the **30-minute `readinessAbsoluteCapFallback`, with no progress watchdog and no caller-visible deadline** (the same gate `pluginCheckLiveVM` re-runs at `live_gather.go:452` before the `ConnectTimeout: 10` `kit.SSHExecutor` reconnect at `live_gather.go:445`). A rebooted guest whose NetworkManager holds **no IPv4 lease** (`STATE:20 unavailable`; ssh `"timed out during banner exchange"`) is therefore indistinguishable from a slow one: the reconnect never bounds, and the bed stalls its whole suite — **observed: the update bed held a 16-lane suite 29+ minutes with no `summary.yml`**.

The CONTAINER arm does not share the defect: `WaitForContainerReady` drives a MONOTONIC poll whose 90-second no-progress watchdog early-outs a stalled venue. The VM arm is the cap-only one, which is why the RCA's stall is VM-only.

**Fix — bound the post-restart reconnect with a TOTAL readiness deadline.** Every post-restart reconnect in the bed sequence now waits under a total deadline and a deadline expiry becomes a **named, time-bounded failure** naming the setup-defect class, so the bed fails fast and the suite proceeds/reports instead of hanging:

- **`candy/plugin-check/bed_readiness.go` (new)** — `rebuildReadyDeadline = 5 * time.Minute` (constant, with the RCA cited in the file header); `waitVenueReady(ctx, deadline, venue, probe)` runs a readiness probe under `context.WithTimeout` (spec/poll's `PollUntil` returns `ctx.Err()` the moment the deadline elapses — **no timer, no sleep, no retry bomb**) and turns `context.DeadlineExceeded` into: `venue <domain> not ready within 5m0s of the post-restart reconnect: … the likely setup defect is a guest booted WITHOUT an IPv4 lease (NetworkManager STATE:20 unavailable, ssh "timed out during banner exchange"), not a slow venue`; `vmSshReadyProbe(domainID)` is the canonical `kit.SSHExecutor` readiness gate (SSH-reachable), bounded per attempt by `ConnectTimeout` and in total by the deadline context.
- **`candy/plugin-check/bed_run.go`** — new `waitRebuildReady` closure (VM arm → the bounded probe; container arm keeps the self-bounding shared gate) wired at the **two post-restart reconnect sites**, each recorded as a `phase` step in `summary.yml` (`rebuild-ready`, `revert-ready`) so the wait's duration and outcome are visible in the run's own evidence: the fresh-rebuild block (was a bare `waitReady()` at the old lines 873/880, now `phase("rebuild-ready", …)` before the local-child redeploy + `check-live-rebuild`), and the anchored `snapshot-revert` + `revert-and-start` site (a revert BOOTS the kept domain — a restart of an already-provisioned disk, the same class).
- **`candy/plugin-check/bed_readiness_test.go` (new)** — the unit gate, through `waitVenueReady`'s own probe seam.

**Why the bound lives in the bed runner (and not in the shared check-live readiness gate).** The deadline is deliberately a POST-RESTART policy: the INITIAL connect legitimately needs the generous window (a first boot provisions the image — pacman/AUR/cloud-init), and only the bed runner knows the venue has just been **restarted**; the shared check-live gate cannot tell a reboot from a first boot, so a global cap there would misclassify a slow first boot. The bounded probe is SSH-readiness only (the reconnect condition); cloud-init settling stays a deploy-time race guard enforced by the downstream check-live gate.

### The two guest `mkinitcpio` advisories and their elimination (R1)

**Mechanism (root cause).** The accept bed's `deploy-add` runs the GUEST's full system upgrade. When that transaction carries a kernel/limine package, the guest's own pacman post-transaction hook rebuilds the UKI (`(13/19) Updating linux initcpios... / Building UKI for linux`) and the guest's `mkinitcpio` prints its advisory `Possibly missing firmware for module: 'xhci_pci_renesas'` / `'qat_6xxx'` lines. On the pre-refresh golden (captured `2026-09-10T23:25Z`) the gate's transaction carried `limine 12.8.0-1 → 12.9.0-1`, so the gate recorded `diagnostics: {errors: 0, warnings: 2}`.

**Remediation EXECUTED in this lane (the golden refresh — not deferred, not an allowlist edit).** The shared golden was re-provisioned and re-captured through the suite's own provisioning bed, which absorbs the pending upgrade into the baseline:
